## 📌 Project Overview
This project serves as an "Instant Briefing" on the shifting landscape of Kenyan financial markets. It leverages **Google NotebookLM** to synthesize a 225-page World Bank/ACBF report on financial inclusion, providing a grounded, high-level summary of the regulatory transition from 2025 into 2026.

The goal of this research is to demonstrate the intersection of traditional finance (Forex/Stocks) and decentralized finance (Crypto/VASP) within the "Silicon Savannah" ecosystem.

## 🚀 Deliverables
- **Executive Summary:** A 5-bullet synthesis of market scale, regulation, and technical pipelines.
- **FAQ Section:** 8 technical questions covering the VASP Act 2025 and leverage risks.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Analysis:** A critical comparison of AI-generated insights vs. manual document verification.
- **Audio Overview:** An AI-generated technical podcast briefing.

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation
This project is built using:
- **NotebookLM:** Used as the RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) engine for grounded research.
- **HTML5 & CSS3:** For the final presentation of the briefing dashboard.

### 🔍 Key Findings ###
  1. The $9.6T Market: Despite being a retail-heavy market, the Kenyan growth is part of a massive $9.6 trillion global turnover.
  2. Regulatory Formalization: The transition from "caution" to the VASP Act 2025 has formalized crypto trading in Nairobi.
  3. The M-Pesa Pipeline: Mobile money acts as the primary Layer 1 settlement bridge for retail traders entering global markets.
 ```
